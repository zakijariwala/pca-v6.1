### B15-D001 | pillars: cost
Scenario: A service runs 24/7 at a steady size and will for 3 years. Which discount fits its baseline?
A) Spot VMs.
B) A 3-year committed use discount.
C) Sustained use discounts only.
D) No discount applies.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>
B. Why: commitments give the largest discount for steady, known use.
A fails: preemption doesn't suit a steady service.
C fails: automatic, but smaller than a commitment.
D fails: discounts apply.
