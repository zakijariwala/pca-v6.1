### B13-D002 | pillars: security
Scenario: Every pipeline shares one broad Cloud Build service account. What's the better practice?
A) A dedicated, least-privilege service account per trigger or pipeline.
B) Grant the shared account Owner to avoid failures.
C) Use a developer's user account for builds.
D) Use a service account key in each repo.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>
A. Why: limits blast radius and makes audits clear.
B fails: widens risk.
C fails: ties builds to a person.
D fails: long-lived keys.
Verified: 2026-09-24
</details>

### B13-D003 | pillars: reliability
Scenario: A team wants a new release to reach 5% of users, watch errors, then expand. Which strategy?
A) Canary.
B) Recreate: stop old, start new.
C) Blue/green with an instant full switch.
D) Rolling update of all instances at once.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>
A. Why: a small share of traffic tests the release before wider rollout.
B fails: all users at once, with downtime.
C fails: all traffic switches together.
D fails: "all at once" isn't gradual.
Verified: 2026-09-24
</details>

### B13-D004 | pillars: reliability, cost
Scenario: A team wants instant rollback by switching traffic back to the old version. It accepts the cost of running both versions during the cutover. Which strategy?
A) Canary.
B) Rolling.
C) Blue/green.
D) Recreate.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>
C. Why: the old environment stays up, so switching back is immediate; it needs double capacity during the switch.
A fails: gradual shift, not an instant switch.
B fails: replaces instances in batches.
D fails: has downtime and no standby.
Verified: 2026-09-24
</details>
