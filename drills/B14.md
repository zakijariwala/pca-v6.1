### B14-D001 | pillars: cost, reliability

A) Refactor.

C) Rehost (lift and shift), modernizing after the deadline.
D) Retire everything.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>
C. Why: moving with little change fits a fixed deadline.
A fails: code changes take longer than four months allow.
